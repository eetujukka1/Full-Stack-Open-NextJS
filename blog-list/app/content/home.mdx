# A very serious place for saving unserious links

Blog list is where useful articles, questionable hot takes, and tutorials with
too many side quests can all live together peacefully. Add a link, give credit
to the author, and let the like count decide whether it was actually worth
everybody's time.

## What this thing does

- Keeps blog links in one searchable-ish place.
- Lets users collect reading-list items without opening seventeen tabs.
- Tracks likes, because democracy needs tiny buttons.
- Gives every blog a detail page so even humble links get a moment.

## House rules

1. Save the article before you forget why it mattered.
2. Like only the posts that deserve future-you's attention.
3. Keep the reading list ambitious, but not legally classified as hoarding.
